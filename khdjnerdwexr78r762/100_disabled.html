100 Questions

1. Please introduce yourself.
Haia im Kaia, at for all intents and purposes, on this website and most places on the internet, that is what I prefer to go by!
2. How long have you been making websites?
I started back in 2022 during my comp-sci class in high-school because my teacher was really boring, and I wanted to do something actual worthwhile.
3. And what got you into the hobby?
When I was looking to do something that would keep me busy in comp-sci, I stumbled across this website, and it really widened it inspired me to make something.
4. What kind of website are you most interested in?
The types of websites that I am interested in definetley have to be ones with a bunch of stuff to read, art and photos to look at, and an interesting person behind the page!
5. What's your workflow? Do you plan your websites out thoroughly or do you come up with the design as you go along?
I am very lost when I make my designs, in the sense that I go through and make my decisions out on a whim. This is probably why I've had many different revisions over the course of this year (2025), but it is really fun that way!
6. Please link to your biggest inspirations.
As mentioned previously, this site by Lu is really what pushed me to see what I could do. I have other inspirations but there would be too many to mention, everything I see inspires me just a little. Each in it's own way.
7. What's your favourite part about making websites?
Definetley sharing stuff about things I love! Getting to express myself, carefree and full of whimsy. :)
8. And the thing you struggle with the most?
I struggle with trying to use new elements and always improve on my last iteration. The learning curve can be rough, but it is very rewarding!
9. Do you keep the same layout on all of your pages? Or do you use different ones?
On all my old pages, aside from the first ever, I would use iframes to frame other pages so the layouts could always be updated with less hassle, but I'm trying to move on from that, just got bored of it! So from this point on, no, I prob wont use the same layout on all my pages, but I (kinda) use to.
10. How confident are you with CSS?
Very-ish! I have gotten much more confident as of recent, but in the beginning i couldnt even figure out how to change the background color of a div T-T
11. Do you know how to correctly use <dl>?
I think this questionnaire is showing me how :sob:
12. What is your favourite HTML element?
I LOVE IFRAMES!!!! So much potential, and super cool. But other than that I really like buttons and lists.
13. If you're making a new web page from scratch, what is the first thing you do?
Figure out the colors I want things, and how I want to lay out any given information. Am I doing the frame referencing thing? What type of things do I share on this page and what things should get their own page? Stuff like that.
14. Do you know JavaScript?
One of the only useful things I actually learned in my comp-sci class that I decided to keep working on. Especially helpful considering that's when I started my indie web-dev journey!
15. How about PHP?
I'm not even gonna lie, I have no idea what PHP is. I think it's an alternative to HTML?
16. Does your website have a theme that you stick to?
Nope! Something different each time. As I'm writing this it's Rosie from animal crossing :3
17. Are you more focused on content or design?
I would say both, equally. I consider the other when working on one.
18. Do you own a domain name? If not, would you ever want to?
I used to own a domain name, hosted at jacobonline.net, but then I stopped paying for it because of a hiatus. Then I came back and hosted on github pages with a subdomain doing a masking thing I think.
19. What do you think of nostalgia-focused or "retro" websites?
I think they're neat! They're incredibly refreshing compared to the flat, bland modern websites that companies make nowadays. Literally anything over that.
20. Is your HTML valid? Do you even check?
Prob is! VS Code doesn't say there are any errors lul.
21. What are your opinion on buttons and banners?
I love banners and buttons, but I typically DIY them. Like on my landing page, the "button" is actually a div for some reason, idk, just the way that I code.
22. What do you think of button walls in particular?
I am a little confused on this question, if talking about 88x31 buttons, I love them! It's like if everyone's site has a little phonebook of interesting people to call. ^^
23. If you started over again, would you make something similar or completely different?
I'm starting over right now! At least while I'm answering this list of questions. I'm postponing finishing the rest of the site overhaul.
24. Are you envious of other people's websites?
No! Everyone is different, and their pages are too. My page will always reflect me, someone else's page will always be unique to that person. It can only ever appreciate a window into someone elses life.
25. What text editor do you use?
Visual studio code! (VS Code). I used to use notepad++ for each page individually. It was not good.
26. Why do you use that one?
Because the extensions can be helpful, and I can customize it! Also, since I host my page on github pages, I can very easily push a commit to update the page.
27. Do you host your image files on your web server, or on another host?
On here! I see no reason not to.
28. This might not be relevant to you, but what's your opinion on the Neocities vs. Nekoweb debate?
Not so relevant to me, I don't really even know what the debate is? But I think both are very useful tools for people who want to build an indie page!
29. How much server space would you estimate your main website takes up?
Considering I store music files on my page as well, probably a lot LOL.
30. Do you keep local backups of your files?
Yes! I do backups every month-ish so that in case something weird with my computer happens (linux moment)
31. Do you prefer simple or highly visual websites?
Both!

I like soft colors and pastels. That and very dark ones, but I am not too fond of oversaturated colors.
33. Have you ever thought about quitting? Why?
