Well, if you want the longer version...

You may have read some of my blog posts in the recent past, and you would know that my mind is... not exactly in the best place. Personally, I felt that this page has grown into an echo chamber of my own mental issues rather than a passion project.

At some point along the development of this, it was no longer an outlet of creativity for me. I kept turning to it for processing emotions that I couldn't really figure out where else to place, other than twitter (gross).

Now, this outcome wasn't really fair to me, or the site, was it? Every page, whether intentional or not, felt like it was being designed with nothing but negative emotions because I trained myself to turn to the site when I felt that way. This felt limiting in what I would let myself do in terms of self-expression, and the product would always be something that could never be interesting to anyone, or myself. It was not healthy.

Thankfully, I don't regret any of what I had created here! Genuinely, some of my favorite little creative projects lie in and around the site, and I am extremely proud of myself for this entire project over the course of 4+ years I have worked on it. But now... I need some distance to remember why I liked doing ANY of this in the first place, y'know..? Instead of unwillingly dragging its corpse with me, and getting it tangled up with everything else going on.

So, that leaves us here. You, reading my frozen words on a webpage, and me, doing better (hopefully). I look forward to creating something new in the future... though, I cannot say when that will be, in all honesty. Moving on from this is important, everything attached to it, names, photos, music, there's stuff here from times of my life that I am not the proudest of, including now. So I hope I can return to this when I'm better. I hope that something beautiful can blossom from the corpse of a webpage dead by my own hand. I hope this can be relatively soon, but only time will tell!
