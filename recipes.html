ginger snaps! (a.k.a molasses cookies)

ingredients:
  • 3/4 cup margarine melted
  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1/4 cup molasses
  • 2 cups flour
  • 2 teaspoons baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground cloves
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground ginger
  • white sugar (for rolling)
steps:
  1. combine all ingredients (except for rolling sugar). combine wet and dry ingredients seperately and then combine both mixtures.
  2. chill the dough for an hour
  3. preheat oven to 375 F
  4. roll dough into like walnut sized balls, then roll them in that white sugar. place onto ungreased baking sheets
  5. bake for 8-10 minutes in the oven, OR until the tops look cracked.
  6. let chill on wire rack
  7. enjoy!

I first made this recipe for an extra credit project in 8th grade. and now, I make them every holiday season for my family! Also my old photography teacher loved these cookies a lot.
