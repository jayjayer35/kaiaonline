Cinnamon Rolls (Cream Cheese Frosting)

these cinnamon rolls i make every holiday season, and they are quite tricky.
but they are so. good.

so heres the recipe! its like a two parter-ish, since you're making the icing too, so this recipe is a time sink, will take you 4/5 hours.

* = for cinnamon rolls
~ = for frosting

Ingredients:
  • 3 1/4 ounces granulated sugar
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 3 ounces butter (soft) unsalted
  • 1 egg
  • 16 ounces of bread flour (flexible)
  • 1 packet INSTANT yeast
  • 8 oz buttermilk
  • 1/2 cup white sugar*
  • 1 1/2 tbsp cinnamon*
  • 1/4 cup brown sugar*
  • 8 ounces cream cheese (room temp) ~
  • 4 ounces butter (room temp) ~
  • heavy cream, to thin out mixture ~
Instructions:

1. Combine 3 1/4 oz. w/ 1/2 tsp salt & 3 oz. of soft butter for 1 min or until fluffy (move into a bigger bowl, you might need it!)

2. Tidy up the bowl and then add 1 egg and mix fast for 2 mins.

3. Add 16 oz bread flour, instant yeast, & 8 oz buttermilk and mix relatively fast for 10 mins

4. In another bowl, oil it around the edges a little, and let the dough sit in a room temperature area to rise for 2 hours or until doubled in size.

5. Knead and roll flat (with rolling pin preferably) to roughly 14x18 inches.

6. Combine the '*' ingredients in a bowl and mix, sifting them togther is optional, but i like the pockets of cinnamon sugar it makes :3

7. Cut into 12 equal slices, or dont, i dont control you, but then ROLL EM UP and lay them on a baking dish evenly spaced with about a 1/2 inch in between them. usually will look like a 4x3 array of small rolls on their sides.

8. Proof for 90 mins (it means let them sit in a room temperature area, covered, and let the dough rise (cause you have yeast in it) for 90 mins)

8.5. You will preheat the oven to 375°F, but since you're letting them proof for an hour and a half, do this when you feel its necessary.

9. Brush the tops with some melted butter if you want, i really recommend it, but it isnt entirely necessary.

10. Bake for 20 mins, or until brown

11. When (or just before) the cinnamon rolls are done, add all the '~' ingredients in a bowl, BUT slowly add heavy cream if YOU feel the frosting is too thick.

12. ENJOY!!! While hot usually, but theyre delicious...

Gingersnap Cookies

these i used to make a lot more during high school around december. i would go around and give cookies to my friends who i feel deserved them LOL, they are pretty good, and are constantly requested every christmas haha

* = cookie coating

Ingredients:
  • 3/4 cup margarine melted
  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1/4 cup molasses
  • 2 cups flour
  • 2 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp ground cloves
  • 1/2 tsp ground ginger
  • 1/2 cup white sugar*
Instructions:

1. Combine the dry ingredients (flour, white sugar, baking soda, salt, and the spices) into a big bowl. Sift them in if you want, not entirely necessary.

2. Combine the wet ingredients (melted margarine, egg, molasses)in a seperate bowl, becareful cause that molasses stuff smells TERRIBLE right now, but it makes the cookie so good, just trust me.

3. COMBINE THEM ALLLLL, then once the dough is nice and uniform, let it chill in the fridge for like and hour.

4. Preheat the oven to like 375°F. Dont worry, you will have to do something while it's preheating, so youll be busy

5. Take the dough out of the fridge and roll them into like, walnut sized balls, and roll them in the sugar* and place onto ungreased baking sheets.

6. Once you've done that (i usually end up with like 36-ish balls of dough), pop them in the oven for 8-10 mins OR until the tops look like theyre cracking.

7. Let cool on a wire rack or whatever you cool cookies on

8. Enjoy the cinnamon-y goodness!
