This site definitely means a lot to me. I originally created it during high school out of complete boredom (and an obsession with a 90s inspired internet). I would update the site in classes that I found pretty boring, but I've been out of high school for a couple years now at the time of writing this, so I mostly maintain this out of passion. After abandoning it for two years, a friend encouraged me to continue the development of the site, so I have worked a year on a rework from the ground up, presenting you with what you're looking at today.

The internet is an important part of me as well; I've learned a lot about myself, the world, people, and much much more. I will never be able to give enough thanks to the internet. To the right (assuming you're on a PC with a big enough monitor and I did everything right) you can see an old photo of me with a traffic cone on my head. Band class was fun.
