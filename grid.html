Combat Grid

Help

Functions

  • You can add a tokens to represent players/enemies or other points of interest.
  • You can draw lines when line mode is enabled. Hold left-click to draw a line, when you let go it will make the line.
  • Erasing can erase both tokens and lines.
  • Resizing the grid will put more squares on the grid.
  • Grid lines can also be toggled with its respective button.
  • Scaling the grid will let you "zoom in/out", please dont go past 70.
  • You can undo line placement/erasure with CTRL + Z.

Info

  • Default scale is 70.
  • Default columns & rows are 20 & 10 respectively.
  • This site was made in a day and probably very very buggy.
  • Made by Kaia for their friends when playing DND.
