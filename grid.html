Help & Shortcuts

Tools

  • Select V - click tokens to select, Shift+click for multi-select, drag on empty space to box-select, drag to move.
  • Token T - opens token creator. Ctrl+click a token on the map to edit it.
  • Wall L - click and drag to draw a wall/line on the grid.
  • Ruler M - drag to measure distance in squares and feet (5ft/sq).
  • Eraser E - click or drag over tokens/walls to remove them.
  • Delete removes selected tokens. Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y to undo/redo.

View

  • Zoom with +/− or Ctrl + Scroll over the map.
  • Hold Space and drag to pan around the map.

Initiative

  • Add rows with +, fill in name and score, then Sort by initiative.
  • Next ▶ cycles turns — any token with a matching name glows green.

Tokens

  • The token list on the left lets you reorder layer depth by dragging entries.
  • Tokens can carry HP/Max HP badges shown on the map.

Saving

  • Save/Load stores in your browser. Export/Import as a JSON file to back up or share scenes.

Info

  • Default 70px/square, 20×10 grid.
