Who is this? Where am I?

If you've somehow stumbled across my site, welcome to the mish-mash of random stuff i feel like sharing with the internet. And (at least on this page) its gonna be about me and this site!

I figured out some github pages stuff with a friend that was more familiar with it, hosted back up my old site files, and then hid the github pages domain with a neat little subdomain so it looks pretty. After that, the rest is history. I'm still working on the site, and I've been recently figuring out how to really do more with it, and some backend stuff for fun (I get notified when people visit me!).

So welcome to my little internet home; ever-changing, and always inspiring me to do better.

Name: Jacob, but when I have a choice: Kaia.
Age: Older than the Nintend DS, but not by a year! OK!?
From: Swampland, USA.
Pets: this little shit, but I used to have two turtles
Fav Song Evar: Owl City's 'Fireflies', so many fond memories of my childhood.
Likes: Baking, coding, linux, rollerblading, video games, my friends, but also being alone, birds, birdwatching, cheese, playing music, listening to music, creating music, making lists, the little things, milk, rhythm games, and myself

The internet is an important part of me; I've learned a lot about myself, the world, people, and much much more. I will never be able to give enough thanks to the internet. Below this text and to the left of it, you can see photos of me! How cool. It's not often I'm gonna show my face here lol.

Originally, I hosted the site with some sketchy free hosting site, but it taught me the basics. After that, I tried to move onto neocities cause I thought it would be fairly easy, but at the time I was developing my page during school to avoid classwork, so neocities just never worked. So after I figured that out, I bought a domain name and hosted all from 'Dreamhost', my domain was jacobonline.net. It taught me even more how all this crazy stuff worked and I did have a lot of fun with it, but after a while I had moved on from my site, stopped paying for my custom domain, and preoccupied with other things in my life, yknow, uni and stuff. Studying aerospace engineering sucked balls, but I met some real cool people at uni. After moving back home, I kept in touch with my super cool uni friends, and one of them I showed my old site files. He loved it and encouraged me to continue working on it! So after some -not so- careful consideration, I dived right back in!
