to my friends:

hello dearest internet friends, real life friends, people on the internet, if we dont communicate often, even if we haven't met eachother- whatever the case is, it will always be fun to share jokes, ideas, and fun times :) thank you guys for everything. we enter this life alone and we leave it alone, but to have you all with me for every other moment in between those couldn't mean more. i feel that i have taken too much for granted. life is short, and we have so much to see and do, and just not enough time. when you choose to spend that time with your friends, it has a meaning that isn't really comparable to anything else. each one of you has a personality, a seperate lifeoutside of mine- yet you make mine feel so special. i can only really hope to provide the same. if you are a stranger on the internet that has come across this: you too, i consider a friend. your time spent on this page, is time spent on me, which is more than enough to consider you one. so, i want you to all know, that you are a reminder of what i have achieved in life, a reminder to push through and continue to fight at whatever may be eating away at inside of me, a reminder that theres always somewhere to fall when i slip. a reminder that there are groups and individuals that i can truly call home, a reminder that i myself am not, and will never, be alone in this.
and for that? thank you.
